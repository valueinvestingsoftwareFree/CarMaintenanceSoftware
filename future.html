Community Requested Features Under Evaluation

The software already includes maintenance scheduling, fuel tracking, mileage monitoring, maintenance history, cost analysis, future maintenance prediction, mobile app integration, and many other vehicle management features.

The following capabilities are currently being evaluated based on feedback and suggestions submitted by users.

Future Development Roadmap

1. Vehicle Reliability Score

Generate a reliability score for each vehicle based on maintenance history, repair frequency, and operating costs.

2. Breakdown Risk Prediction

Estimate the probability of future failures based on mileage, age, and historical maintenance patterns.

3. Total Cost of Ownership Analyzer

Calculate the real long-term cost of owning a vehicle including fuel, maintenance, repairs, insurance, and depreciation estimates.

4. Vehicle Replacement Advisor

Help determine whether maintaining a vehicle or replacing it may be the more economical decision.

5. Maintenance Priority Dashboard

Identify which maintenance tasks require immediate attention and which can safely wait.

6. Future Maintenance Cost Forecast

Estimate expected maintenance expenses over the next months and years based on historical records.

7. Multi-Vehicle Fleet Dashboard

Monitor maintenance status, costs, mileage, and upcoming tasks across multiple vehicles from a single screen.

8. Tire Lifecycle Management

Track tire purchases, rotations, replacements, mileage usage, and estimated remaining lifespan.

9. Battery Health Monitoring

Track battery age, replacement history, expected lifespan, and replacement recommendations.

10. Maintenance Cost Benchmarking

Compare maintenance spending across vehicles and identify unusually expensive vehicles.

11. Annual Vehicle Cost Report

Generate yearly reports showing fuel, maintenance, repair, and operating expenses.

12. Maintenance Trend Analysis

Visualize maintenance spending trends over time with advanced charts and statistics.

13. Service Interval Optimization

Analyze maintenance history to recommend optimal service intervals for each vehicle.

14. Vehicle Health Score Dashboard

Provide an overall vehicle condition score based on maintenance compliance and repair history.

15. What-If Maintenance Simulator

Evaluate the financial impact of delaying, accelerating, or modifying maintenance schedules before making decisions.
