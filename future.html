Future Development Roadmap

1. Vehicle Health Score

Generate an overall vehicle condition score based on maintenance history, mileage, repair frequency, and operating costs.

2. Lifetime Cost of Ownership Analysis

Calculate the true lifetime cost of owning a vehicle, including fuel, maintenance, repairs, insurance, and depreciation.

3. Tire Replacement Planner

Track tire wear, estimate remaining lifespan, and forecast future replacement dates and costs.

4. Vehicle Replacement Analyzer

Estimate when replacing a vehicle may become financially more beneficial than continuing repairs.

5. Fuel Price Impact Simulator

Analyze how changing fuel prices affect annual transportation expenses.

6. Breakdown Risk Estimator

Estimate the probability of unexpected repairs based on vehicle age, mileage, and maintenance records.

7. Annual Vehicle Cost Forecast

Project future ownership costs for the next several years.

8. Multi-Vehicle Comparison Dashboard

Compare maintenance costs, fuel efficiency, reliability, and total ownership expenses across multiple vehicles.

9. Resale Value Tracking

Monitor estimated vehicle value and depreciation trends over time.

10. Preventive Maintenance Optimizer

Identify maintenance actions that may reduce future repair expenses and extend vehicle lifespan.

11. Costliest Components Analysis

Determine which vehicle systems generate the highest long-term maintenance expenses.

12. Future Repair Fund Planner

Estimate how much should be saved monthly to prepare for future maintenance and repair costs.

13. Vehicle Aging Analysis

Analyze how maintenance frequency, reliability, and operating costs change as vehicles age.
